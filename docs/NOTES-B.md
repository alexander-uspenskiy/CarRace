# NOTES-B (physics / AI / race director)

Reading of ambiguous spec points and deliberate deviations. All numbers are knobs: `TUNING` (carPhysics.js), `AI_TUNING` (aiDriver.js).

- **Extra files** (allowed extras): `sim/carStep.js` (one car's per-step pipeline, shared by RaceSim and solo runs), `sim/soloRun.js` (headless single-car runs, used by calibrate + tests). `RaceSim.setPlayerAutopilot(on, {pace})` takes an optional pace (default 0.96); `config.autopilotPace` is also honoured.
- **Racing line**: pure min-length relaxation (spec) hugs the inside of tight corners and gives a line *tighter* than the centreline (breaks the "max |kappa_line| <= max |kappa_centre|" test). It is a min-curvature (bi-Laplacian) SOR, 800 iterations, plus the 0.15 m/sample offset clamp. Speed profile uses a g-g aware backward/forward pass (lateral demand reduces braking/traction), `brakeForceLimit()` is shared with the AI.
- **ABS split consequence**: the 0.6/0.4 brake split saturates the front at ~15 kN, so any steering while braking harder than that has no front grip. The AI (and its profile) brake at most `brakeForceLimit(u, ay)` while cornering.
- **Tyre wear**: `load` normaliser is `TUNING.wearLoadNorm = 1.25` (spec 1.6) so a normal-difficulty AI stint ends at 0.52-0.63 wear (spec target 0.55-0.65); hard ends ~0.7-0.8.
- **Grid slot `s`**: `buildGrid(config, teams, rng, trackModel?)` returns `s` wrapped when a trackModel is passed, else the signed distance behind the line (`-(10+8*slot)`).
- **Ordering rule** while some cars have the flag: finished cars sort by `lapsCompleted` desc then `finishTime`; an unfinished car counts as `floor(progress/L)+1` laps minus 0.5, so a lead-lap car still running ranks ahead of a lapped car that already crossed.
- **Surface**: car is 'grass' when >= 2 wheels are beyond the kerb, else 'kerb' if any wheel on the kerb.
- **Throttle at launch**: the AI's throttle is force-controlled (traction circle), so at standstill the pedal value is small (~0.06) although the car is traction-limited; physics matches the spec formula.
- **Overtaking** (deviation from "always pass when closing"): AI only starts a pass when held up > 1.5 s by a genuinely slower car, with >= 260 m of straight before the next braking zone, nobody else passing within 45 m, lateral offset <= 3.2 m from the line; passes abort before braking zones/sweepers. Alongside avoidance moves at most 1.5 m. Stuck/wrong-way AI (and an autopilot player) are reset to the track after 4 s / 6 s.
- **Calibration** (`node tools/calibrate.mjs --write`): measured with the final AI: monte-veloce 66.1, ridgeback 67.7, costa-marina 71.8 s. Re-run after any change to tracks/physics/AI. NOTE the winner of a 5-minute race finishes ~13% early (rounding to 4 laps + leader pace); the default test allows +-15%, `FULL=1` hard runs +-20%.
- Tests: `node --test "tests/sim/*.test.js"` (~9 s); `FULL=1` adds all tracks x {5,7,10} min x {easy,normal,hard} (~45 s).
- **Finish/cut-off fix (QA bug: 19 cars `finished:false, lapsDown:1` after the leader's flag)**: root cause was `D.shouldEnd()` ending the race `PLAYER_FINISH_GRACE` (3 s) after the *player* finished, so an autopilot/leading player cut every other car off seconds before the line. Now the race ends only when all 20 cars have crossed the line, or at flag + `cutoffSeconds(referenceLapTime) = max(90 s, 1.6 x referenceLapTime)` (director gets `referenceLapTime` from RaceSim). Each car finishes at its next line crossing after the flag (`lapsCompleted`, `finishTime` set; snapshot `finishTime` null only for unfinished cars); classification = laps desc, then time. `playerTotalTime` = player's own `finishTime`. Spec §4.7 "player finished + 3 s" is superseded. Cut-off only now matters for stuck/stationary cars. Test: `tests/sim/finish.test.js` (default: monte-veloce 10 min + 5 min; `FULL=1`: 3 tracks x 5/7/10 min x easy/normal/hard — all 20 finished:true, positions 1..20, ordering, finishTime in snapshot).

### Shapes / APIs that changed or grew (UI/render/game: all additive, nothing renamed)
- **Event `car:reset`** `{carId, reason:'stuck'|'offtrack'|'wrongway'|'manual', auto:boolean, x, y}` (new; `game.js` forwards sim events 1:1 so it is on the bus; the UI can show "Car reset"). Not in spec §3.
- **`CarSnapshot.ghost`** 0..1 (new; >0 for ~2.5 s after a reset, car-car contact is off; render may fade the car). `CarSnapshot.u` can now be **negative** (reversing); `speed = hypot(u,v)` stays >= 0 (wheel spin / HUD speed unchanged, gear shows 1).
- **Reverse is now supported** (spec 4.3 said no): brake held (no throttle) with the car stopped for 0.3 s -> reverses at 5 m/s^2, capped at 8 m/s; releasing the brake rolls it to a stop (12 m/s^2). Steering is natural (kinematic model). `TUNING.reverse*`.
- `CarStats` gains `heatLimit` (derate threshold, 0.8 base). `config` gains `expectedSeconds`. `computeLaps(minutes, ref, difficulty='normal')`, `estimateRaceSeconds(laps, ref, difficulty='normal')` take an optional difficulty (UI callers unchanged -> 'normal'). `stepThermal(car, stats, dt, draft=0)`, `resolveWall(car, model, stats, dt=1/120)`, `runSolo({..., onStep})`. `describeUpgrade('cooling', L)` returns 3 lines (was 2). `LENGTH_CREDIT_MULT` is now `{5:1, 7:1.45, 10:2.14}` (UI reads it from data; `ui/dataRefs.js` only has a fallback copy of the old values).

### 1. Stuck player (QA: 1.8 episodes/min under random input, median 21 s)
Root causes found: (a) at crawling speed the front tyre's steering scrub (`-Fyf*sin(delta)`) was still applied while the lateral dynamics were overwritten by the kinematic model, so a car at full lock + throttle on grass / against a wall could not accelerate at all (u stalled at 0.05 m/s); fixed by fading that term out below 6 m/s. (b) A nose-first car cannot yaw at standstill: `resolveWall` now pushes 2 cm past the wall and, while the driver is on the throttle within 1.5 m of the barrier at < 6 m/s, swings the body about its CG towards the nearest track-parallel direction (1.1 rad/s). (c) Reverse (above). (d) **Auto-recovery for every car incl. the human** (`RaceSim.recoverCar`, `RECOVERY` knobs): after `startGrace` 4 s of racing, speed < 2 m/s for > 5 s (AI: 4 s), or > 2.5 m beyond the kerb for > 8 s, or wrong way > 8 s (AI 6 s) -> teleport to the track centre at the nearest point (`car.s`), facing forward, speed 0, 2.5 s ghost, 1 s reset cooldown (R still works right after), `car:reset` event. A manual R (last good pose, 3 s cooldown) now also emits `car:reset`.
Result (qa_06 fuzz, 200 random-input runs of 60 s): **0 of 200 runs with the player stationary > 8 s (QA baseline: 197 of 200)**, 0 failed resets, no NaN/escape/exception; the longest stationary stretch is ~5 s (the recovery timer). A stationary AFK player sits on the racing line after a reset, so the AI now passes **parked cars** (u < 4 m/s, in its lane, from 150 m, any speed/straight rules) - needed for the existing "stationary player still ends the race" test.

### 2. Upgrades (solo autopilot, pace 0.96, no mistakes, mean of the 3 tracks; level 5 vs 0, best / mean lap)
| category | before | after (best / mean) | mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| engine | -1.5% | **-1.9% / -1.8%** | power +5%/lvl (was 4), boost +9%/lvl |
| aero | -3.0% | **-1.95% / -1.9%** | downforce +3.8%/lvl, drag +1.2%/lvl |
| brakes | 0.0% (+0.05) | **-0.83% / -0.77%** (mv -1.1, rb -0.95, cm -0.5) | stock 12 500 N (was 21 000, never the limiter), +14%/lvl (L5 21 250 N); AI trust: `brakeConfidence` (0..1) raises the AI corner-speed margin 0.92 -> 0.99 and braking-zone share 0.90 -> 0.98 |
| tyres | -4.9% | **-2.2% / -2.9%**, wear **x0.59** (was x0.66) | grip +0.7%/lvl, wear x0.9^L |
| cooling | 0.0% | **-0.48% / -0.55%** (mv -0.7, rb -0.2, cm -0.5; more in traffic) | see below |
| **all five** | -10.2% (QA: 8-12%) | **-8.0% / -8.5%** (mv -10.6, rb -7.0, cm -6.4) | |
Physical limit worth knowing: braking is only ~10% of a lap and even a +70% brake force is worth only ~0.4% of lap time (profile sensitivity measured), so the rest of the brake gain comes from the AI confidence coupling; for a human the benefit is real: 80 m/s -> 0 takes 28% less distance at level 5 (128 m vs 179 m; test). Brakes/cooling stay the cheapest-effect categories at the same price (costs are global in `UPGRADE_COSTS`, not per category).
**Cooling mechanic** (`tyres.js stepThermal`, `carPhysics.js heatDerate`): heat gain `heatGain*throttle (+0.08/s while boosting)`; cooling is Newtonian, `coolRate * airflow(speed) * (0.25 + 0.75*heat/0.8)`, so heat settles to an equilibrium instead of running away, and airflow is cut by up to 30% in a slipstream (dirty air). Above `heatLimit` (0.8, +4%/level -> 0.96) the engine **loses up to 8% torque** (smoothstep over +0.2 heat); ERS boost cuts out at `heatLimit + 0.12`. Cooling level: heat gain -5%, cooling +12%, threshold +4% per level. A stock car sits above its limit 80-97% of a solo stint (AI boosts until heat 0.95: `AI_TUNING.boostHeatMax`), a level-5 car never does (peak heat 0.4-0.7). `car:overheat` now fires once per excursion above 1.0 (re-armed below 0.7, at most every 8 s; was ~40/race of spam). `statSummary.cooling` = `coolRate/heatGain*heatLimit`.
Races (autopilot player at pace 0.96, 9 normal races): all-max wins 9/9 (pace 0.92: 8/9), stock finishes P2-P4. The car is ~8% quicker than the best AI, so winning is still easy at good pace; lowering `UPGRADES` further would be the knob.

### 5. Save loader
`coerceNum`: finite numbers and numeric strings ("4321") everywhere the save / HoF reads numbers (credits, levels, settings, stats, `last.minutes/driverIndex`, HoF times and counters); garbage ("lots", "", "12px") still falls back to the default; credits clamp to 0..999 999 999. `version` is real: `runMigrations(raw, steps, current)` (core/storage.js) runs `MIGRATIONS[v]` v -> v+1 (missing/invalid version = 0; versions >= current are left alone and field-sanitised; a failing step stops the chain, never throws); `MIGRATIONS[0]` in saveStore maps the unversioned prototype format (`name`, `money`/`coins`, `racesRun`, flat `upgrades`, `settings.sound`) to v1, in hallOfFame (`records`, `races`) likewise; loading a pre-current save writes the upgraded copy back. `sanitizeName` truncates by grapheme (Intl.Segmenter, `Array.from` fallback), drops lone surrogates left by older truncated saves. When `SAVE_VERSION` is bumped: add `MIGRATIONS[1]` and a test in `tests/data/saveStore.test.js`.
